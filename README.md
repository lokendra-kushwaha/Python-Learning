My workspace is divided into dynamic categories to keep my growth organized:

*   **`01_Python_Masterclass`**
    The foundation. This folder contains the code, notes, and experiments I write while learning core Python concepts and new syntax.
    
*   **`02_Core_Implementations`**
    Practice makes perfect. A collection of daily scripts, exercises, and challenges I solve to sharpen my logic and problem-solving skills.
    
*   **`03_Python_Logics`**
    Under the hood of Python. Here, I focus on algorithmic thinking by decoding and recreating Python's built-in functions from scratch to deeply understand internal mechanics and time complexities (like O(N)).
    
*   **`04_Math_Algorithms`**
    Where mathematics meets programming. A space dedicated to translating complex math into functional code. It includes custom Object-Oriented engines (like Linear Algebra operations, Matrices, Vectors) and will continue to grow as I tackle more advanced math.
    
*   **`05_My_Python_Programs`**
    Real-world applications. This section houses my full-fledged software projects, tools, and games, categorized into their own subfolders.
